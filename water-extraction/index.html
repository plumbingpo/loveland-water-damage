Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Loveland Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Loveland using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
